# User Notes / Research

## Assumptions
- Kids will tolerate short delays but not silent waiting.
- A simple hotkey interaction is workable for ages 5–10 (with parent setup).
- Parents want fewer interruptions, not necessarily perfect translations.

## Questions to validate in friend testing
- Which is worse: wrong answer, slow answer, or noisy/long answer?
- Do kids naturally ask good questions, or do they need prompting?
- Does TTS voice quality and speed matter more than content?

## Friend-test survey (draft)
1. What game did you test?
2. How long did setup take?
3. Did the app ever feel “stuck”?
4. Did the child understand how to use the hotkeys?
5. Were answers too long, too short, or about right?
6. Any moment where the app made the experience worse?
